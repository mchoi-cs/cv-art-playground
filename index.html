cv‑art‑playground

Small browser toys that point a webcam at an art problem. Everything runs on your own machine — the video never leaves the device, and there is no backend to speak of.

  • Demo 1 · live

    Head lighting study

    A faceted planes-of-the-head bust copies your head pose in real time. Move the light around it, change its colour, and drop into Rembrandt, split, loop, butterfly or rim lighting with one click — so you can see how light falls on the pose you are actually holding.

    Open the study →
  • Demo 2 · coming soon

    Hand gesture animations

    Map MediaPipe hand gestures — open palm, fist, thumbs up, victory, pointing up, I love you — onto hand-drawn animation loops, so a gesture in front of the camera plays the drawing made for it.
